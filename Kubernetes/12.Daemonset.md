## 1. What is a DaemonSet?

### Question

**What is a DaemonSet in Kubernetes?**

### Explanation

A **DaemonSet** ensures that a copy of a Pod runs on **every eligible node** in the Kubernetes cluster.

For example, if your cluster has 3 nodes:

Node 1 → FluentD Pod
Node 2 → FluentD Pod
